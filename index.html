    Your operational playbook, encoded.
    
        
    Generic AI tools run on one flat prompt. We capture the rules your best people follow by instinct, whether that's an estimator, a dispatcher or a service writer, and model how conditions combine.
    
        "You are a helpful assistant. Book the customer's appointment."
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
          
          
        
    
        
    
        
    3-tab shingles over 15 years old, in a zip code that just took hail, go to an insurance claim instead of a repair quote.
    
        
    A leaking water heater gets a shutoff walkthrough first. Once the water is off, a 12-year-old tank books the first morning slot with a replacement quote, sparing the customer an emergency rate. Water still spreading, or a gas smell, gets the on-call tech or the gas company right away.
    
        
    A car already booked for brakes, 6,200 miles past its last oil change, gets the oil change offered on the same visit and added to the repair order. A soft pedal or pulling skips the upsell and goes straight to a service advisor.

      FAQ
      
    Questions owners ask us.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    What does DeployAI actually do?
    We learn how customers reach you and how jobs get booked, find the biggest leak, and fix it with AI and automation connected to the software you already use. Then we keep it running.
    
      
    Will it sound robotic?
    You hear the voice and approve the script before anything goes live. The demo calls on this page use the same kind of neural voices.
    
      
    Can callers still reach a person?
    Yes. Urgent or complicated calls go to you or your team, based on rules you set, like an active leak or a safety issue.
    
      
    What if it gets something wrong?
    Every call and booking creates a record you can see. We review calls each month and fix anything that was routed or booked wrong.
    
      
    Do I need to change my phone number or software?
    No. It works with your current number and the software you run today. If your software can't connect, we'll tell you before you pay anything.
    
      
    What does it cost?
    It depends on what we fix. After a 15-minute consultation you get a fixed quote: a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee.
