Construction

IdeaAI (steel) meets the business (navy) at a clean seam. The seam opens the word in the D and closes it in the “I”, both at 45°.
DDrawn on a 70-unit cap height, stem 11.5 u (matches Outfit Medium). Steel triangle with 36 u legs, extending into the D's white center. Seam gap 3.5 u.
“I”Outfit SemiBold. 45° seam starting 42 u above the baseline on the left; the navy cap is about 40% of the letter. Seam gap 3 u.
Wordmark“eploy” in Outfit Medium, “l” ascender trimmed to 74 u. “AI” in Outfit SemiBold, tightened 2 u. All letters are outlines; never retype the logo in a font.
Small sizesBelow 160 px wide use the small-size version. App icons at 64 px and below use the small-size icon (gap ×1.8).

Clear space and minimum size

  • Clear space on every side: half the cap height (the height of the D’s triangle leg).
  • Full logo: 160 px wide minimum on screen, 40 mm in print. Small-size version: 110–160 px.
  • Mark alone: 16 px minimum.
