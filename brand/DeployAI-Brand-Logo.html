DeployAI logo: G3 · Single Slice

Selected mark. A solid D cut by one 45° slice: the steel piece is AI meeting the navy business at a clean seam. The mark is built into the DeployAI wordmark as its D. Wordmark: Jost Medium, outlined, with "AI" in steel.

Concept

One cut instead of two keeps the mark simple and calm. The steel top-left piece represents AI and data; the navy body is the client's business. The 45° seam is where DeployAI does its work: the integration between them.

  • Fewest pieces, cleanest at 16px
  • Two-tone feels premium and precise
  • Easy to reproduce in any medium

Logo

The G3 mark replaces the letter D, so the name reads DeployAI with a single D. The mark alone is used for the app icon, favicon and other tight spaces.
